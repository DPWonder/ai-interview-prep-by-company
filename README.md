## Apriora (now Alex) AI interview: how to prepare

What Alex's own public pages say about its AI interview, each line with the page it comes from and the day we read it. Aethel is a separate company and is not affiliated with Alex.

| | What Alex's own pages say | Source (day read) |
|---|---|---|
| Format | live as a conversation over video, phone or chat | [alex.com](https://www.alex.com/candidate-faq) (2026-11-19) |
| Follow-ups | asks follow-up questions on your answers | [alex.com](https://www.alex.com/candidate-faq) (2026-11-19) |
| Length | an AI interview usually takes 15 to 30 minutes | [alex.com](https://www.alex.com/blog/how-to-ace-an-ai-interview-a-candidates-guide) (2026-11-19) |
| What the employer gets | shares the recording and the transcript with the hiring team | [alex.com](https://www.alex.com/candidate-faq) (2026-11-19) |
| Who decides | humans make all hiring decisions | [alex.com](https://www.alex.com/candidate-faq) (2026-11-19) |
| Feedback | Alex cannot give you feedback on your interview | [alex.com](https://www.alex.com/candidate-faq) (2026-11-19) |

**How to prepare for an Alex AI interview**

- **Book the hour when you are sharpest.** Alex's FAQ says you choose the time from the link in the email and can move it with that same link. Pick a rested hour in a quiet room, and move the slot if the day goes wrong rather than taking it in a rush.
- **Open the link on your device the day before.** Use the device you will interview on, allow the camera and microphone in the browser, and check your connection. Alex's FAQ suggests at least 10 Mbps WiFi or 5G, and says a paused interview can be restarted.
- **Match the job post to your own projects.** Alex's guide says answers are scored against a rubric of skills the employer set for the role. Write down the skills the job post names and match each one to a project of yours with a result you can name.
- **Say each story in four parts.** Alex's guide suggests STAR: the situation, your task, what you did, the result. Say each story out loud in about a minute, name the real tools, and add a number where you have one.

| Questions | unique and based on your profile, your experience and your answers in a dialogue with Zara | [intercom.help](https://intercom.help/alignerr/en/articles/10288644-alignerr-interview-faqs) (2026-11-16) |
| Device | you need a laptop or desktop with a working webcam | [intercom.help](https://intercom.help/alignerr/en/articles/10288644-alignerr-interview-faqs) (2026-11-16) |
| AI tools | any use of AI tools, outside resources or outside help is strictly prohibited during the interview | [intercom.help](https://intercom.help/alignerr/en/articles/10288644-alignerr-interview-faqs) (2026-11-16) |
| Retakes | a retake is currently allowed only if technical difficulties | [intercom.help](https://intercom.help/alignerr/en/articles/10288644-alignerr-interview-faqs) (2026-11-16) |

**How to prepare for the Alignerr AI interview with Zara**

- **Reread your own profile first.** Alignerr says Zara's questions are based on your profile and experience. Read what you submitted, line by line, and be ready to give one concrete example for each skill or job you listed.
- **Choose what you can and cannot talk about.** Before the call, decide which past projects are under an NDA. Alignerr says you can ask Zara to skip such a question, so have a short sentence ready, such as saying the work is under an NDA and offering another example.
- **Explain your field to a careful listener.** The interview checks what you know about your topic. Practice explaining one idea from your field in plain words, then one decision you made and why, then a mistake you caught and how. Short, exact answers say more than long general ones.
- **If your field is coding, warm up on live coding.** Alignerr says coding assessments can include live coding. Solve two or three small problems with a timer in the language you listed, saying out loud what you are doing as you go.

| Who decides | humans decide who advances | [usebraintrust.com](https://www.usebraintrust.com/products/air) (2026-11-18) |
| Option without AI | an alternative led by humans is available when the employer requests it | [usebraintrust.com](https://www.usebraintrust.com/how-braintrust-air-stays-compliant) (2026-11-18) |
| Languages | 16 or more languages | [usebraintrust.com](https://www.usebraintrust.com/products/air) (2026-11-18) |

**How to prepare for a Braintrust AIR AI interview**

- **Read the AI notice, and ask for what you need first.** Braintrust says candidates are told before the interview that it is led by AI and recorded. Read that notice. If you need an accommodation or want the alternative led by humans, write to the employer before you start, since Braintrust says those requests go through the employer.
- **Find the skills the rubric is likely built on.** Braintrust says answers are scored against the competencies the employer sets for the role. Read the job post, list the skills it names, and pick one piece of your own work for each, with a result you can state.
- **Tell each case in four parts, out loud.** Braintrust's tips suggest the STAR method: the situation, your task, what you did, and the result. Practice saying each case in about a minute, with the real names of tools and a number where you have one.
- **Welcome the follow-up question.** Braintrust says a short or generic answer likely brings a follow-up. When one comes, give the specific example it asks for instead of repeating your first answer in other words.

| What is scored | skills are mapped to a 1 to 5 scale | [codesignal.com](https://codesignal.com/ai-interviewer/) (2026-11-17) |
| Who decides | humans review and decide | [codesignal.com](https://codesignal.com/ai-interviewer/) (2026-11-17) |

**How to prepare for a CodeSignal AI interview or AI-assisted coding interview**

- **Ask which CodeSignal AI interview you have.** This page covers two CodeSignal formats: the AI Interviewer, a spoken conversation, and the AI-assisted coding interview in its IDE with Cosmo. If your invitation does not say which, ask the recruiter. They need different practice.
- **For the AI Interviewer, give complete short answers.** CodeSignal says it probes thin or vague answers. Name a real case, your own part, what you chose and why, and the result, in about a minute. Then a follow-up adds depth instead of filling a gap.
- **Match a story to each skill in the job post.** CodeSignal says follow-ups stay within the competencies the employer defines. Read the job description, list the skills it names, and pick one piece of your own work for each, with a second detail ready if asked.
- **Pick your moment and your room.** CodeSignal says you can take the AI Interviewer on any device, at any time. Choose a quiet room, a time with 20 minutes or more free, and test your microphone first. The hiring team can get the audio and the full transcript.

| Length | the employer can change the interview's sections, questions, scope and duration | [support.hackerrank.com](https://support.hackerrank.com/articles/7398460680-customize-an-ai-interviewer-in-chakra) (2026-11-16) |
| Before you start | candidates grant access to the webcam, microphone, screen sharing and multiple-monitor detection | [support.hackerrank.com](https://support.hackerrank.com/articles/5542727476-invite-candidates-to-an-ai-interview) (2026-11-16) |
| What is scored | what they say in the conversation and the work they do in the hands-on section | [hackerrank.com](https://www.hackerrank.com/chakra) (2026-11-16) |
| Free mock interviews | one Software Engineer coding mock and one Technical Screen mock, each taken once | [help.hackerrank.com](https://help.hackerrank.com/articles/8988753946-introduction-to-mock-interview) (2026-11-16) |

**How to prepare for a HackerRank AI interview with Chakra**

- **Find out which hands-on round you have.** HackerRank describes two: a coding question in an editor, or a Code Repository question where you direct an AI agent in a real codebase. If your invitation or recruiter does not say which, ask. They need different practice.
- **Think out loud while you code.** Chakra talks with you during the task and asks about your approach afterwards. Practice solving a small problem in a set time while saying what you are trying, why, and what you would test next. HackerRank says it scores what you say as well as the work, so say your reasoning.
- **Practice in the editor setup you will have.** Pick one language you know well and solve a few short problems in a plain browser editor with a timer: read the task, write a first version, run it, fix one thing, and stop on time. Speed comes from habits, not from memorising answers.
- **For a repository task, practice steering an agent.** HackerRank says it judges how you frame tasks, steer the agent and judge its output. Practice writing one clear instruction, reading the diff before you accept it, running the tests, and saying out loud why you kept or rejected a change.

| Practice questions | three general practice questions you can repeat as often as you like and watch back | [hv4-candidate-help-center.brainfish.ai](https://hv4-candidate-help-center.brainfish.ai/articles/practice-your-interview-faq-cVFASdrFAD) (2026-11-12) |
| Who decides | the hiring decision is made by people at the company | [hirevue.com](https://www.hirevue.com/candidates/faq) (2026-11-12) |

**How to prepare for a recorded HireVue interview**

- **Read the invitation first.** Find the interview type (On Demand or Live), the due date and the recruiter's contact. Those three answer most questions later, because HireVue sends deadline, retake and access questions back to the employer.
- **Practice on the device you will use.** Run the setup check and the practice questions on the same laptop or phone, in the same room. Watch one answer back for light, sound and how close you sit, then fix one thing at a time.
- **Write five short stories from your own work.** For a developer or new graduate: a bug you tracked down, a feature or project you shipped, a review comment you disagreed with, a deadline you missed or nearly missed, and something you learned fast. Note your part, the reason for each choice, and a result with a number where you have one.
- **Rehearse against a timer.** Answers have a preparation countdown and then a recording countdown of a few minutes. Practice using the preparation time to pick a story and three points, then answering in about two minutes with a clear ending.

| Browser | recommends the latest Google Chrome, supports Microsoft Edge and Safari, and does not support Firefox | [talent.docs.mercor.com](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/support/ai-interview) (2026-11-12) |
| What you need | a computer whose camera and microphone both work, your whole face in view, and a test of both before you begin | [talent.docs.mercor.com](https://talent.docs.mercor.com/support/ai-interview) (2026-11-12) |

**How to prepare for the Mercor AI interview**

- **Take Mercor's own Practice Interview.** It is free, it does not affect your applications, and it lets you try Mercor's own interview before the official one, so start there.
- **Set up the way Mercor asks.** Use a computer with Chrome, a working camera and microphone, headphones, and a quiet room. If your interview needs screen sharing, be ready to share the entire screen. Run the waiting room checks before you start.
- **Choose three cases from your own work.** A problem you solved, a decision you made, and a time you explained something or worked through a disagreement. For each, know where it was and how big, what you did yourself, why, and how it turned out, with a number if you can.
- **Say them out loud.** Tell each case in about two minutes, then in one. Practice not pausing for too long mid-answer, and if the interviewer steps in early, say you were not finished.

| Same questions for everyone? | no two candidates get the same questions | [micro1.ai](https://www.micro1.ai/interview-questions) (2026-11-12) |

**How to prepare for the micro1 AI interview with Zara**

- **Pick the one skill you are strongest in.** micro1 asks you to pick a single core skill or type of job, and its questions follow that choice. Choose the area where you have the most real work to talk about, not the widest one.
- **Run micro1's free practice first.** Its Interview Prep tool lets you rehearse with an AI interviewer for the position you picked, so the real conversation is not your first one.
- **Build a short case list.** Write down four pieces of your own work: something you built, something that broke and how you fixed it, a decision with a tradeoff, and a time a plan changed. Note the size, your part, your reason and the result for each.
- **Practice explaining while you work.** If your role has a coding challenge, rehearse solving small problems in a set time and be ready to explain your choices. If it has a data exercise, practice saying how you would judge the quality of an answer before you start.
